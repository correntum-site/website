Timeslot Business connects your WhatsApp Business number, shows your client chats in one place, and helps you keep track of bookings. You can disconnect your number at any time. In your WhatsApp Business app, go to Settings, then Account, then Business Platform, and tap Disconnect. You can also email us and we will disconnect it for you.

When your number is disconnected, we stop receiving and sending your WhatsApp messages and we delete the connection details and access key. The chats and bookings already in Timeslot Business stay in your account until you delete them or delete your account.
