Privacy policy for Timeslot Business

Version 1.0 · Effective [DATE]

Timeslot Business is an app for small businesses, like barbers and trainers, that take bookings on WhatsApp. It connects the business's WhatsApp Business number, shows their client chats in one place, and helps them keep track of bookings.

This policy is for two groups of people:

  • Providers. The businesses that sign up for Timeslot Business.
  • Clients. People who message a provider on WhatsApp. Clients do not sign up for anything with us.

We explain each group separately below.

Who is responsible for what

We are responsible for providers' information. Each provider is responsible for their own clients' information, and we handle it only on that provider's behalf and on their instructions.

In the words of the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA): we are the responsible party for providers' information, and the provider's operator for their clients' information. We do not use clients' information for our own purposes, we do not use it for marketing, and we do not sell it. We keep each provider's information separate from every other provider's.

Information leaves South Africa

Our servers and database are in London, in the United Kingdom. So your information is sent outside South Africa. The other companies above process it in the countries shown in the table.

POPIA allows this when the company that receives the information is bound by a law, binding corporate rules or a binding agreement that gives it adequate protection (section 72(1)(a)). Each of these companies (Vercel, Supabase, Meta, Anthropic, Expo and Postmark) is bound by a data processing agreement that does this. For providers' information, the transfer is also necessary to perform our contract with providers.

How long we keep it

  • Providers. While your account is open. When you ask us to delete your account, we erase it 30 days after we confirm your request.
  • Clients. While the business uses Timeslot Business. It is erased when the business deletes their account, or when the business or you ask us to delete it. We verify each request and complete it within 30 days. We do not yet delete old messages automatically after a fixed time.
  • Money records. If a booking was paid for, we keep the amount, date and payment reference, without names or contact details, for at least 7 years, because company and tax law require it.
  • Proof of deletion. When we erase an account, we keep a note that it was erased, with internal ID numbers and dates and no name or contact details.
  • Backups. We take an encrypted backup of the database once a week and keep only the 4 most recent. Information you delete stays in older backups until they are replaced, at most about 8 weeks. If we ever restore a backup, we erase again anyone who had already been erased.
  • Other copies. Meta keeps messages for at most 30 days. Postmark keeps emails for 45 days. Our server logs are kept for 1 hour.

Deleting information from Timeslot Business does not delete the chat in the business's own WhatsApp app or on your phone.

How we protect it

Information travels over encrypted connections. Passwords are stored only as hashes. Backups are encrypted. Only the people who run the service can reach the database. If someone gets unauthorised access to personal information, we will tell the Information Regulator and the people affected, as POPIA requires.
