How to delete your data from Timeslot Business

Version 1.0 · Effective 15 October 2026

Timeslot Business is made by Correntum (Pty) Ltd. There are two sets of steps: one for businesses that use the app, and one for their clients who message them on WhatsApp.

If you are a business using Timeslot Business

Steps

  1. If you still have bookings in the future, cancel or finish them first.
  2. Email support@correntum.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it.
  3. Our team checks that the request comes from you, then starts the deletion.

How long it takes

Your account is hidden as soon as we start the deletion, and erased in our nightly run once 30 days have passed.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email address, password and settings.
  • Your business profile, services and hours.
  • Your WhatsApp connection details.
  • All your clients' information: their names and numbers, all messages, what the assistant understood, bookings, and the activity record of your account.
  • Your push notification and email records.

What is kept, and why

  • Money records. If a booking was paid for, we keep the amount, date, service and payment reference for 7 years, with no names or contact details, because company and tax law require it.
  • Proof of deletion. A note that your account was erased, with internal ID numbers and dates only, so we can show we did it.
  • Backups. Your information stays in our encrypted backups until they are replaced, at most about 8 weeks.
  • Copies we do not control. Meta keeps WhatsApp messages for at most 30 days. Postmark keeps our emails for 45 days. Your chats stay in your own WhatsApp app.

Only disconnecting WhatsApp

To stop Timeslot Business reading your WhatsApp without deleting your account, open your WhatsApp Business app and go to Settings, then Account, then Business Platform, and tap Disconnect. WhatsApp should then stop passing your messages to Timeslot Business. Your connection details, chats and bookings stay in your account until you delete it.

If you message a business that uses Timeslot Business

You do not have an account with us. The business you messaged is responsible for your information, and we handle it for them.

Steps

  1. Ask the business you messaged to have your information deleted from Timeslot Business. They pass the request to us.
  2. Or email support@correntum.com with:
    • the name of the business you messaged, and
    • your WhatsApp number.

We may ask you to show that the number is yours. We tell the business about your request, and our team deletes your information by hand, on the business's instructions.

How long it takes

There is no fixed time yet, because our team does this by hand. Everything about you is erased when the business deletes its account.

What we can delete

  • Your name and WhatsApp number as that business's client.
  • Your messages with that business, and the business's replies to you.
  • What the assistant understood from your messages.
  • Your bookings with that business.

What is kept, and why

  • The business's activity record. Timeslot Business keeps a record of the messages and actions in the business's account, which can include your messages. It cannot be changed, so it stays until the business deletes its account.
  • Money records. If you paid for a booking, the amount, date, service and payment reference are kept for 7 years, with no name or contact details, because company and tax law require it.
  • Backups. Your information stays in our encrypted backups until they are replaced, at most about 8 weeks.
  • Copies we do not control. Meta keeps WhatsApp messages for at most 30 days. Your chat stays in the business's own WhatsApp app and on your phone. Ask the business if you want them to delete it there.

If you message the business again after we delete your information, the new messages reach Timeslot Business like any other message.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will put the new version on this page with a new version number and effective date. If a change is material, we will also tell providers by email or in the app.

Questions

Email support@correntum.com. Our privacy policy explains what we collect and why.
