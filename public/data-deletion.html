How to delete your data from Timeslot Business

Version 0.1 (draft). Effective date: [[MISSING: effective date]].

Timeslot Business is made by Correntum (Pty) Ltd. There are two sets of steps: one for businesses that use the app, and one for their clients who message them on WhatsApp.

If you are a business using Timeslot Business

Steps

  1. In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account.
  2. Confirm, then sign in again to prove it is you.
  3. Tap the final button to request deletion. The app shows the date your account will be erased.

Or email support@correntum.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it.

If you still have bookings in the future, cancel or finish them first. Until then the request is refused.

How long it takes

Your account is hidden from the moment you ask, and erased 30 days later, in our nightly run.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email address, password and settings.
  • Your business profile, services and hours.
  • Your WhatsApp connection details.
  • All your clients' information: their names and numbers, all messages, what the assistant understood, bookings, and the activity record of your account.
  • Your push notification and email records.

What is kept, and why

  • Money records. If a booking was paid for, we keep the amount, date and payment reference for 7 years, with no names or contact details, because company and tax law require it.
  • Proof of deletion. A note that your account was erased, with internal ID numbers and dates only, so we can show we did it.
  • Backups. Your information stays in our weekly encrypted backups until they are replaced, at most about 8 weeks.
  • Copies we do not control. Meta keeps WhatsApp messages for at most 30 days. Postmark keeps our emails for 45 days. Your chats stay in your own WhatsApp app.

Only disconnecting WhatsApp

To stop Timeslot Business reading your WhatsApp without deleting your account, open your WhatsApp Business app and go to Settings, then Account, then Business Platform, and tap Disconnect. Or email us. We then delete the connection details and stop receiving your messages.

If you message a business that uses Timeslot Business

You do not have an account with us. The business you messaged is responsible for your information, and we handle it for them.

Steps

  1. Ask the business to delete your information from Timeslot Business. They can do this in the app.
  2. Or email support@correntum.com with:
    • the name of the business you messaged, and
    • your WhatsApp number.

We may ask you to show that the number is yours. We tell the business about your request and delete your information on its behalf.

How long it takes

Within 30 days of confirming your request.

What is deleted

  • Your name and WhatsApp number as that business's client.
  • All your messages with that business, and the business's replies to you.
  • What the assistant understood from your messages.
  • Your bookings with that business, and the activity record entries about you.

What is kept, and why

  • Money records. If you paid for a booking, the amount, date and payment reference are kept for 7 years, with no name or contact details, because company and tax law require it.
  • Backups. Your information stays in our weekly encrypted backups until they are replaced, at most about 8 weeks.
  • Copies we do not control. Meta keeps WhatsApp messages for at most 30 days. Your chat stays in the business's own WhatsApp app and on your phone. Ask the business if you want them to delete it there.

If you message the business again after we delete your information, the new messages reach Timeslot Business like any other message.

Questions

Email support@correntum.com. Our privacy policy explains what we collect and why.
